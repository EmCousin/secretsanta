    Install on your device

    Use your browser's Install or Add to Home Screen menu. On iPhone or iPad, open Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. Menus vary by browser.

    Open the app online once and wait until it's ready for offline use. Each new device needs this first visit. Browsers can clear saved files, so keep your links somewhere safe. You still need a connection to send invitations.

    Reload to update?

    Save your exchange or copy the invitation links first. Reloading will discard unsaved changes on this page.

    You can use this without an account or installing anything. The draw stays on your device.

    About your privacy

    This site has no application backend or analytics. Invitations carry their data in the part of the link after #, which isn't sent to the static host in an HTTP request. The host still receives normal website requests. Anyone holding a link, including the organiser or messaging provider carrying it, may read its contents. Links are encoded, not encrypted or verified. Saving a copy is optional and local to this browser.
