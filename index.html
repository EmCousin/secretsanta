    About your privacy

    This site has no application backend or analytics. Invitations carry their data in the part of the link after #, which isn't sent to the static host in an HTTP request. The host still receives normal website requests. Anyone holding a link, including the organiser or messaging provider carrying it, may read its contents. Links are encoded, not encrypted or verified.
